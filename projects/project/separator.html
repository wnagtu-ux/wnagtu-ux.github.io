设计理念

Design Philosophy

集结构创新、智能感应、防堵防漏、人居适配于一体，重塑现代家庭厨房油污处理新体验。

Integrating structural innovation, intelligent sensing, anti-clogging and leak prevention, and human-centered design, it reshapes the modern family kitchen oil stain treatment experience.
