东航客运大飞机座椅

一套能覆盖多种体型乘客与多类出行场景的座椅方案。

组长 · 2025.03 – 2025.07 · 校企合作项目 · 与航空公司合作

设计理念

Design Philosophy

在效率至上的航空业，我们重新思考对乘客差异化需求的精准响应。通过解决静脉曲张乘客的舒展、带婴家长的行李收纳、高大旅客的座椅调节及年长者的通行难题，为有限客舱的乘客提供体贴舒适的体验。

In an efficiency-oriented aviation industry, we are rethinking the precise response to passengers' differentiated needs. By solving the stretching needs of passengers with varicose veins, luggage storage for parents with infants, seat adjustment for tall passengers, and the transportation difficulties for the elderly, we provide a considerate and comfortable experience for passengers in limited cabins.
